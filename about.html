Meet the band

We are a group of friends from Waterford who are passionate about all things 80’s. We love music and dancing the night away, and know there’s nothing better than live music to get the party going!

Eva Nowak

Eva has been singing as long as she could talk. Her love for the stage started in her school choir. She is the lead singer of Retro Riot and also performs solo as a wedding singer.

  • @evanowak
  • @evanowaksings

Fred Byrne

Fred loves guitar and rocking out with his friends. He teaches guitar full-time and uploads guitar covers to YouTube. He is the guitarist and supporting vocalist for Retro Riot.

  • Fred Byrne Guitar School
  • @fredbyrneguitar

Maya Carberry

Maya is a DJ, producer, and sound engineer. She has a degree in music technology with a MA in creative music technologies. She has performed at festivals and produced for major labels including Warner Music. She is the sound engineer and drummer for Retro Riot.

  • @themayacarberry
